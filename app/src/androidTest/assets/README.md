# Audio fixture

`qa-96k-24bit.flac` is six seconds of two generated low-frequency square-wave signals,
96,000 Hz, 24-bit, stereo. The sample values include a nonzero low bit. It contains
no music or personal media. It validates native high-resolution FLAC decoding,
duration and seeking through the production playback service, without an account
or network. Emulator decoding does not prove hardware or headset output fidelity.
